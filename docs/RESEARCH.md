# 000345.com — Research & Concept Decision

_Prepared October 2026._

## 1. What "000345" means in commerce, business, AI, the economy and to people

| Context | Finding | Usable for a site? |
|---|---|---|
| Stock / security codes | Six-digit codes beginning `000` are the Shenzhen A-share format; Hong Kong uses `00345` for Vitasoy International Holdings. The number is an identifier, not a brand anyone can build on — and using it as a finance site invites trademark/affiliation confusion. | **No** — confusion risk, low trust, YMYL scrutiny for AdSense |
| Product / part / registration codes | "000345" appears as generic catalogue, part, error and reference numbers with no dominant owner. | No — no search demand, no audience |
| AI / tech | No standard model, protocol or dataset named 000345. | No |
| People / culture | "345" reads as a sequence; "000" reads as a starting point or zero-state. | Weak alone |
| **Construction & math** | **3-4-5 is the most widely used field method for squaring a corner** (Pythagorean triple: 3² + 4² = 5²). It is taught in carpentry, used on foundations, decks, framing, tile and landscaping, and scaled as 6-8-10, 9-12-15, etc. | **Yes — a real, evergreen, high-intent audience** |

**Conclusion:** the only meaning of 000345 with durable search demand and commercial intent is **"start at zero, square it with 3-4-5."** That maps the domain to the construction / home-improvement layout-and-estimating niche.

## 2. Why this niche wins on revenue

- **AdSense CPCs are high.** Construction-estimating keywords show US CPCs around $13–$17 ("construction estimator" ≈ $13.27, "estimating for construction" ≈ $16.76). Calculator queries such as "construction cost calculator online" show ≈ $9, "build cost calculator" ≈ $9.
- **Lead value is very high.** Home-improvement lead marketplaces charge contractors roughly **$15–$85+ per shared lead** (Angi Leads), with dynamic $35–$60 typical on Thumbtack; Facebook-ad leads cost contractors $40–$120. A site that already has homeowners measuring their project is sitting on lead inventory.
- **Market size.** US home improvement was reported at about $574B in 2024, growing ~3.5% a year.
- **Calculator traffic is sticky and evergreen.** Tool pages earn repeat visits, bookmarks and backlinks, and don't decay like news.

### Revenue model (assumption-driven, for planning — not a forecast)

| Stream | Assumption | Monthly at 50k pageviews |
|---|---|---|
| AdSense display | RPM $8–$15 in home-improvement, US/CA-heavy traffic | $400–$750 |
| Lead generation | 0.4% of visits submit a request (≈ 200); sold to 2 pros at $20 avg | ≈ $8,000 gross if a buyer network exists |
| Sponsorships | 2 calculator/category sponsors at $250–$750 | $500–$1,500 |
| Donations | Small, mostly signal value | $50–$200 |

The decisive lever is **lead generation**, not ads. AdSense pays the hosting and proves traffic; the contractor network is the business. Without signed contractor buyers, leads are worth $0 — recruiting pros (the `pros.html` funnel) must start on day one.

## 3. Competitor audit — 29 sites visited

Omni Calculator, Inch Calculator (home + concrete page), Calculator.net, CalculatorSoup, The Calculator Site, GIGAcalculator, RapidTables, Blocklayer, RoofingCalc, Homewyse, Angi, HomeAdvisor cost hub, Thumbtack, Bark, Fixr, HomeGuide, Networx, Modernize, Houzz, Porch, ConcreteNetwork, This Old House, Family Handyman, Bob Vila, BuildingAdvisor, Buy Me a Coffee, GoFundMe, Patreon (+ Instructables contests listing).

### Patterns adopted on 000345.com
1. Interactive 3-4-5 squaring tool as the hero (unique to this domain — no competitor leads with it)
2. Live-recalculating calculators with imperial/metric switch and per-field help text (GIGA, Inch Calculator)
3. Results in large type, with formula + worked example on every page (Inch Calculator)
4. In-content "Get quotes for this job" CTA pre-filled with project type (Inch Calculator, RoofingCalc)
5. ZIP-first, 4-step quote form with progress bar, one theme per step, express consent checkbox (Angi, Modernize, Bark)
6. Separate "Join as a pro" contractor funnel with area, trade, volume cap, insurance attestation (HomeGuide, Networx)
7. Editorial byline, last-reviewed date, methodology page (Bob Vila, GIGA)
8. Long-form guide content + FAQ schema on every tool (AdSense / E-E-A-T)
9. Related calculators + contextual internal links
10. Print / share actions (The Calculator Site, Blocklayer)
11. Embedded YouTube how-tos, click-to-load privacy mode (Family Handyman, ConcreteNetwork)
12. Donation amount presets with purpose selector and supporter wall (Buy Me a Coffee, Patreon)
13. Monthly contest with prizes, judging rubric and official rules (Instructables)
14. Sponsor tiers and media-kit request (ConcreteNetwork, Bob Vila)
15. Dark mode, skip link, keyboard focus, responsive single-column mobile (Inch Calculator)
16. Safety/local-code disclaimer on structural tools (Blocklayer)

### Not adopted (and why)
- Phone OTP verification (Bark): needs a paid SMS backend; add once lead volume justifies it.
- City-level cost pages (HomeAdvisor): high value but needs real local cost data — Phase 7 roadmap.
- Fabricated stats/testimonials: deliberately excluded until real numbers exist.

## 4. Positioning
**000345 — the square-corner toolkit.** Free layout and estimating calculators built around the 3-4-5 rule, with a homeowner → contractor quote funnel. Brand promise: *Start at zero. Square it with 3-4-5.*
