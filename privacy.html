Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

Summary

Calculators run in your browser and we don't store what you type into them. We collect personal information only when you send it to us through a form. We use Google AdSense to show ads, which uses cookies.

Information you give us

When you request quotes, apply as a contractor, enter a contest, apply for a job, pledge support or contact us, we receive what you enter in the form (such as your name, email, phone, postal code and project details). Forms are delivered to us by email through the FormSubmit relay service.

How we use it

  • Quote requests: to match your project with up to three contractors, who receive your details so they can contact you. We do not sell your information to list brokers.
  • Other forms: only to respond to you and run the related program (contest, application, sponsorship, donation).

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Our cookie notice lets you choose non-personalised ads. In regions where a consent management platform is required, consent is collected through Google's certified consent tools.

Local storage

We store your theme, unit system and cookie choice in your browser so the site remembers them. This never leaves your device.

Embedded videos

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and only load after you press play. Once loaded, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Retention and your rights

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you sent them for, and no longer than 24 months. You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, through the contact page. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or PIPEDA), you may have additional rights, which we honour.

Children

This site is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the age required by local law).

Changes

We'll post any changes on this page with a new date.
