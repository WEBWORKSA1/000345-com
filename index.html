Why 000345?

Every build starts from a zero point — a corner stake, a ledger end, the first tile line. From there, three units one way and four the other, and a diagonal of exactly five, gives you a right angle you can trust. That sequence, 0-0-0-3-4-5, is the whole idea of this site.

We turn that idea into tools: calculators that square corners, size slabs and footings, lay out stairs and rafters, and count boards, tiles, bricks and paint. Each one explains its method so you can check the math yourself.
