Support 000345

Every calculator here is free, with no account and no paywall. Reader support and sponsors pay for hosting, new tools, video production, contest prizes and the people who build and check them.

Where your support goes

Operations

Hosting, domains, security and the time it takes to keep every formula checked and current.

New tools & content

Hiring tradespeople and writers to build new calculators and review existing ones.

Contests & prizes

Funding the monthly Square Corner Challenge and its prizes.

Promotion

Reaching more builders and DIYers through outreach, video and partnerships.

Choose an amount

Pick a one-time or monthly amount and leave your details. We'll reply with a secure payment link. You can also give anonymously.

000345 is not a registered charity; contributions are not tax-deductible.

Sponsor the project

Brands, suppliers and contractors can sponsor a calculator, a contest month or the video library. Sponsors are clearly labelled and never change our calculations.
