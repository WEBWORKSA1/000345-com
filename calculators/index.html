Construction calculators

18 free tools for layout, concrete, framing, roofing, decks, fences and finishes. Imperial and metric, with formulas and worked examples on every page.

Layout

3-4-5 Squaring Calculator

Find the exact diagonal for a square corner and check a measured one.

Solve any right triangle from two sides: hypotenuse, angles, area.

Compare two diagonals to prove a frame, slab or room is square.

Square Footage Calculator

Area of rooms and L-shapes, in ft² and m², with cost.

Board feet, cubic volume and cost of lumber.

Deck Board Calculator

Fence Calculator

Finishes

Tile Calculator

Tiles and boxes for floors, walls and backsplashes.

Flooring Calculator

Boxes of laminate, vinyl plank or hardwood plus underlayment.

Paint Calculator

Gallons or litres of paint and primer for a room.

Drywall Calculator

Sheets, tape, compound and screws for a room.

Landscape

Gravel, Mulch & Soil Calculator

Cubic yards, tonnes and bags of gravel, mulch, topsoil or sand.
