By the 000345 editorial team · Last reviewed October 2026 · How we calculate

Most rooms are rectangles or combinations of rectangles. Break the space into pieces, measure each, and add them together. A bay or angled wall can be handled as a triangle (base × height ÷ 2).

Measuring tips

  • Measure at floor level, wall to wall, and round up to the next inch or centimetre.
  • For an L-shaped room, split it into two rectangles that do not overlap.
  • 1 m² = 10.764 ft².

Once you know the area, take it to the flooring, tile or paint calculators.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the square footage of an L-shaped room?

Divide it into two rectangles, multiply length by width for each, and add the results.

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

Divide square feet by 10.764. To go the other way, multiply square metres by 10.764.
